**The best way to test it (about 30 minutes)**

1. Pick a deal you already know well: one you've closed or underwritten
   recently, where you know what's wrong with the rent roll.
2. Upload its lease PDFs (scans are fine), then import the rent roll.
   Use the **Excel or CSV** export from your property management
   system, and type the property address the way it appears on the leases.
3. Open **Deal Mismatch Report** and compare what it found with what
   you found by hand. Did it miss anything? Flag anything that isn't real?
4. If you have the T-12 (Excel or CSV), add it under **Cross-Check
   Against a T12** on the Upload page to check collections against the rent roll.

There's a **Help & Guides** section in the left sidebar. "Getting
started" walks through all of this in about five minutes.

**Two things to know about the beta**

- **Keep your source files.** This test server can lose uploaded files

  Nothing is wrong on your side.
