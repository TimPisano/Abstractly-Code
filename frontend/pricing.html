FAQ

Pricing questions we'd ask too

How do contracts work?

You sign either a monthly or an annual contract — use the toggle above to compare the two. Annual contracts are billed upfront at a discount off the monthly rate; monthly contracts are billed every month at the standard rate. Pricing is flat per team, not per property or per document.

Is there a pilot?

Yes — early testers get a free pilot before committing to a paid plan. Ask us during your demo to set it up.

How accurate is the extraction?

We're not going to hand you a made-up accuracy percentage — anyone can print a number, and it means nothing without knowing what it was measured against. Every extracted value shows a confidence level and links back to the exact page and quote it came from, so you or your analyst can verify any figure in seconds rather than trusting a black box.

How is our data secured and isolated?

Your documents and the data extracted from them are stored under your account and are never shared with, or resold to, a third party. Each team's documents and results are only visible to that team's members. You can delete any document and its extracted data at any time, and no credentials or secrets are ever stored in our codebase — only in environment configuration.

Do you use our documents to train AI?

No.
