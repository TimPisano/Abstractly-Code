How It Works

Built For Section 8

Built for voucher portfolios, not office leases

Most lease tools were built for commercial real estate. Section 8 is a three-party deal — you, the tenant and the housing authority — and every unit's rent comes from two payers.

Two payers per unit

Tenant share and HAP portion are tracked separately, so a short HAP payment never hides behind a full tenant payment — and a rent roll that books the tenant share as the whole rent gets flagged.

Inspections and recertifications

PHA inspection and annual recertification dates drive what you get paid. A missed reinspection can mean an abatement; a recert that changes the tenant share has to reach the rent roll. Keep each unit's dates in view so neither slips.

Many housing authorities, one view

Every PHA has its own forms, notices and payment schedule. Work with several, and see each unit's housing authority, HAP contract and payments side by side instead of in separate portals and spreadsheets.

FAQ

Questions we'd ask too

What happens to our data — including tenant information?

Your documents and the data extracted from them are stored under your account and are never shared with, or resold to, a third party. You can delete any document and its extracted data at any time. If you export to Google Sheets, that data leaves our system and lives in your own Google account, under Google's own terms — we don't control it once it's there.

How accurate is the extraction?

We're not going to hand you a made-up accuracy percentage — anyone can print a number, and it means nothing without knowing what it was measured against. What we do instead: every extracted value shows a confidence level and links back to the exact page and quote it came from, so you (or your analyst) can verify any figure in seconds rather than trusting a black box.

Which documents and systems do you support?

PDF leases, HUD tenancy addenda, HAP contracts and PHA notices, including scanned/image-only PDFs (via OCR) and files that bundle several documents into one, plus Excel/CSV rent rolls and direct import from Yardi, AppFolio, RealPage, MRI, and Buildium exports.

We work with several housing authorities. Does that matter?

That's the point. Each unit is tied to its housing authority and HAP contract, so payments and notices from different PHAs are checked against the right documents and shown together. We read what the PHAs send you; we never submit anything to a PHA or HUD on your behalf.

Does this replace our property management system?

No. Your PMS records what should happen; Abstractly checks it against the leases, the HAP contracts and what the housing authority actually paid — so your team spends its time resolving gaps, not retyping a rent roll line by line to find them.
